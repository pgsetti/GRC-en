# GRC Series

A practical, series on Governance, Risk and Compliance applied to cybersecurity.
The goal is simple: take a subject most people find boring and show why it actually decides whether a security program works or dies.

## Who this is for
- People starting out in GRC and not sure where to begin
- Technical folks who got handed "the documentation problem"
- Managers and sponsors who need to understand what they're approving
- Students who learn by building, not by reading

No prior GRC experience required. Every post builds on the previous one, so you can follow along and assemble your own process as the series goes.
